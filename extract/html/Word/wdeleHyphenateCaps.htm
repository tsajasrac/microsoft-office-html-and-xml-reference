DoNotHyphenateCaps Element (Microsoft Word)

Specifies that Word does not hyphenate words that are all uppercase.

Contained In

WordDocument

Remarks

If <w:DoNotHyphenateCaps/> is specified, the uppercase words are not hyphenated.

See Also

AutoHyphenation, ConsecutiveHyphensLimit, HyphenationZone
